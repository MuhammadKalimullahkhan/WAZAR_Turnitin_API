Research and write an essay on the ethical implications of data collection and privacy in the digital age. This assignment could explore a range of topics related to data privacy, such as surveillance, data breaches, and the use of personal information for advertising purposes. Students could be asked to reflect on the ethical considerations that arise when personal information is collected and used, and to consider the potential consequences of different policies and practices.

Develop a privacy policy for a fictional company that collects user data. This assignment could involve students in developing a privacy policy for a company that collects user data, such as an e-commerce site or a social media platform. The policy should cover topics such as what data is collected, how it is used, and how users can access or delete their data. Students could be asked to think critically about the impact of different policy choices on user privacy and to consider the trade-offs between privacy and other goals, such as revenue generation.
